CoolCare HVAC: AI Lead Qualification & CRM Automation

Website development, workflow automation, lead qualification and CRM management for a Dallas-based residential and commercial HVAC company. It is a self-initiated sample project, tested with sample leads.

Overview

From website inquiry to a sorted CRM lead

A customer-facing HVAC website is connected to an automated back office. Visitors browse services and submit a service request. An n8n workflow validates each lead, checks the service area and urgency, and creates the contact in GoHighLevel, where tags make every lead easy to identify, filter and prioritize.

1 · Website development

A professional first impression and clear next steps

The site presents CoolCare as a Dallas-based provider of residential and commercial HVAC service and gives visitors an obvious next step, whether they prefer to call or request service.

2 · n8n workflow automation

Receive, validate, qualify and route

The workflow receives each website inquiry through a webhook, processes the lead data in a JavaScript code node, validates the submission, checks the service area, identifies emergency requests and routes the lead to the matching GoHighLevel node. Leads that fail validation are flagged for human review instead of being lost.

  • Webhook (POST) intake, then a Code in JavaScript step and a Validation check
  • Valid leads are marked as qualified, then checked against the service area
  • Separate paths for in-area and outside-area leads, each with an emergency-urgency check
  • Dedicated GoHighLevel nodes: In-Area Emergency, In-Area Normal, Outside-Area Emergency, Outside-Area Normal and Needs Human Review
  • All branches finish at a shared HTTP Request node
3 · GoHighLevel CRM integration

Contact records and tags that classify every lead

Each lead is stored as a GoHighLevel contact with name, phone, email, created date and tags. The tag set classifies leads by qualification status, urgency and service area, and also covers the appointment and customer stages, so staff can filter and prioritize at a glance.

  • Qualification status: ai-qualified, ai-needs-review
  • Urgency: urgency-emergency
  • Service area: outside-service-area
  • Source and stages: source-website-form, appointment-requested, appointment-booked, customer-serviced
