Lead Generation and Booking Automation for MAIDUP House Cleaning Services

A GoHighLevel build for MAIDUP House Cleaning Services, a Los Angeles home cleaning business: a landing page, quote form, CRM pipeline and follow-up automation that help turn quote requests into booked estimate calls.

Project details
  • Client: MAIDUP House Cleaning Services
  • Timeline: September–October 2026
  • Platform: GoHighLevel
  • Deliverables: Landing page, quote request auto-reply, CRM pipeline setup and booking follow-up workflows
The problem

Quote requests that go cold

Many local cleaning companies take quote requests by phone, text or a basic contact form, with no instant reply and no way to book. Leads wait, and some book with whoever answers first.

What I built

Landing page, CRM form and booking

  • Conversion-focused landing page built in GoHighLevel, with a clear headline and call to action, service information, customer reviews and trust-building sections, and service area and contact details
  • Quote form (name, email, phone, address, service, home size) tracked as a lead in the GoHighLevel CRM
  • CRM pipeline that tracks each lead from quote request through booking, no-show and lost
  • Thank-you page with an embedded GoHighLevel booking calendar for a 15-minute free quote call
  • Mobile-friendly layout
Automation

Three workflows

Each workflow scrolls down automatically so you can see all of it. Hover to pause, or click to enlarge.

Objective

Fewer manual follow-ups, more booked estimates

The goal was a more organized and automated lead management process: capture quote requests, keep leads organized in one pipeline, reduce manual follow-ups, and make it easier for a potential customer to go from requesting a quote to booking an estimate call.
