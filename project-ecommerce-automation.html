Project features

What the system does

Automated customer order collection

  • Customers submit orders through Google Forms.
  • Captures customer and product information.

Automated order processing

  • Validates submitted order details.
  • Generates unique Order IDs.
  • Checks available product inventory.
  • Calculates order totals automatically.

Inventory management

  • Deducts stock automatically after valid orders.
  • Records every inventory movement.
  • Updates product inventory status.

Inventory alert automation

  • Low Stock, Insufficient Stock, and Out of Stock alerts.
  • Automated administrator email notifications.

Order status management

  • Dropdown statuses: Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered, Completed, and Cancelled.
  • Copies completed orders to a separate Completed Orders sheet.
  • Sends a customer delivery confirmation email when the status becomes Delivered.

Dashboard

  • Total, pending, and completed orders, and total sales.
  • Total products and total inventory stock.
  • Low stock products, insufficient stock orders, and out of stock products.

Email marketing automation is not part of this project yet.

Workflow overview

From order to notification

When a customer places an order

  1. Customer Order FormGoogle Forms
  2. Apps Script validationChecks the order details
  3. Order creation and inventory deductionOrder ID, total, stock update
  4. Google Sheets recordsOrders and Inventory Logs
  5. Inventory alertsLow, insufficient, out of stock
  6. Zapier and Gmail notificationsAdmin alerts and customer email

When the admin updates an order

  1. Admin updates order statusDropdown in the Orders sheet
  2. DeliveredStatus changes to Delivered
  3. Customer delivery emailSent through Zapier and Gmail
  1. CompletedStatus changes to Completed
  2. Completed Orders sheetThe order is copied to its own sheet
