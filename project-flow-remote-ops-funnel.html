The offer

Who it is for and what they get

The page speaks to business owners who are growing but still doing everything themselves. Its headline is "Your Business Is Growing. Why Are You Still Doing Everything?" and the offer is a free, written Delegation Roadmap, not a generic PDF. It states clearly that the page is for owners looking to delegate, not for job seekers.

  • The primary bottleneck in the business
  • 3 to 5 tasks worth delegating
  • A support category and priority level for each task
  • A practical first step
  • Questions to resolve before hiring

The funnel also says plainly that a process fix may be the better answer than a hire, and that there is no obligation to work with Flow Remote Ops afterward.

Funnel flow

From visitor to roadmap request

  1. HeroHeadline, promise, and a clear call to action
  2. Problem and valuePain points, then why a roadmap comes before hiring
  3. Example and calculatorAn illustrative roadmap and an optional hours calculator
  4. Assessment formEmail, website, support type, and biggest challenge
  5. Follow-up or bookingA written roadmap, or a call booked through Google Calendar
Lead form

A short assessment form

The form asks for just enough to prepare a useful roadmap.

Work email

Required. Where the roadmap and follow-up go.

Company website

Required. Lets the team review the business before replying.

Type of support needed

Optional dropdown: executive and admin, finance and bookkeeping, creative and content, systems and automation, lead generation and sales support, customer support, or not sure yet.

Biggest operational challenge

Required. The main problem the roadmap should address.

The form also includes a hidden spam-trap field and a consent note about being contacted and requesting data deletion.

Features

What is on the page

Problem list

Admin overload, inconsistent lead follow-up, scheduling drag, delayed client work, unclear hiring priorities.

Illustrative example

A sample roadmap, clearly labeled as an example and not an actual client result.

Hours calculator

Visitors list recurring tasks and weekly hours. The page totals weekly hours and estimates monthly hours, and says it is not a savings estimate.

How it works

Three steps: tell us about your business, we review it, you receive written recommendations.

FAQ

Cost, deliverables, follow-up, no obligation to hire, support categories, and how this differs from a generic VA.

Book a call

A closing call to action with booking through Google Calendar.

Skills shown

What this project demonstrates

  • Funnel and offer design. A single-goal page that moves a visitor from problem to offer to form.
  • Lead capture. A short form with required and optional fields, a spam trap, and a consent note.
  • Front-end development. Responsive HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, including an interactive hours calculator.
  • Deployment. Hosted on GitHub Pages and connected to a booking calendar.
